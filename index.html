Harvest Hollow

Grow a rundown plot into a thriving homestead. Till, plant, water, and harvest through the four seasons — mind the weather, raise animals, and make the valley bloom.

Made with code — no assets, no libraries.

How to Play

WASD / Arrows — Move
Space / Click — Use tool
1–5 — Select tool
Q E — Cycle tools
Tab — Change seed
B — Shop
Enter — Sleep (ends day)
Esc — Pause

The rhythm of a day

① Hoe grass to make soil. ② Plant a seed. ③ Water it. Crops grow one stage each night — but only if watered that day. Skip watering and your crop dries out and dies. Rain waters everything for free.

Each crop only grows in its season. Sleep to pass the night, restore energy, and advance your farm. Sell your harvest at the Shop, then reinvest in seeds, land, tools, and animals.
